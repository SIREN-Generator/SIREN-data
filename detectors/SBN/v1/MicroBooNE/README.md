# MicroBooNE detector geometry

## microboonev12_nowires.gdml

Geant4/LArSoft GDML of the MicroBooNE liquid-argon TPC and its LArTF building,
the MicroBooNE production geometry (`microboonev12`, the geometry selected by
`ubcore/Geometry/geometry_microboone.fcl`) in the `nowires` variant: the 8,256
sense wires are omitted while every other volume is kept, including the
sensitive `volTPCActive`, the 32 PMTs and the CRT panels. Used by SIREN's
`load_detector("SBN", detector="MicroBooNE")`.

### Geometry description

LArSoft detector coordinates (cm): the origin is at the anode plane, the TPC
mid-height and the upstream TPC face; +x is the drift direction (anode to
cathode), +y up, +z along the beam. The world is an Air box of
1483.26 x 1060 x 1483.26 m; its placements, listed with their positions, are

- `volDetEnclosure` at (128.175, 0, 518.5): the Air interior of the LArTF pit
  and hall (a boolean of the pit cylinder and the above-grade cylinder of
  radius 7.4118 m), holding
  - `volCryostat` (LAr) at the enclosure origin: a horizontal cylinder of
    radius 1.9161 m and length 10.8649 m closed by spherical domes (sphere
    radius 3.0525 m, half-angle 38.88 deg, centred 3.056 m from the middle),
    12.2 m overall, containing `volSteelVessel` (11.1 mm of
    `STEEL_STAINLESS_Fe7Cr2Ni`, 7.93 g/cm3, radius 1.905 to 1.9161 m and the
    matching dome shells), `volUllage` (34.3 cm of `Argon_gas_87K` at the top
    of the tube), `volTPC` (LAr, 260 x 256 x 1045 cm at (0, 0.97, 0) with the
    cathode plate, field cage and wire-plane frames) and, inside it,
    `volTPCActive` (LAr, 256.35 x 233 x 1036.8 cm at (-1.55, 0, 0));
  - `volInsulation`: 0.4064 m of `PU_foam_light` (0.0384 g/cm3) on the tube,
    radius 1.9171 to 2.3235 m;
  - 32 `volPMT` assemblies (LAr envelope with acrylic plate, glass stalk and
    underside, stainless base, lens; `volOpDetSensitive` plate) behind the
    anode plane, 4 acrylic paddles, the platform, columns, racks, floor tanks
    and pumps;
  - the CRT modules (`volModule_*_strip_*`, polystyrene strips);
- `volConcreteEnclosure` (pit wall, inner radius 7.4168 m, 0.4572 m thick,
  11.5824 m tall at y = 0.4572 m), `volConcreteEnclosureBottom` (0.9144 m
  floor at y = -5.7912 m), `volPolystyreneEnclosure` (5.08 cm outside the
  wall), `volConcreteWallAboveGrade` (8.9916 m above grade),
  `volRoofPlusBars` (roof disc, I-beams and cross beams, rotated 39 degrees,
  at y = 13.97 m), all at (128.175, y, 518.5);
- `volGround` (Dirt, 1.7 g/cm3, radius 7.9248 to 23.114 m, height 12.4968 m,
  centred at y = 0, so grade is 6.2484 m above the TPC centre) and
  `volGroundBottom` (radius 23.114 m, 15.24 m tall, below it);
- `volVacuumSpace` at y = 262.25 m: a 1483.26 x 512 x 1483.26 m box of
  `Vacuum` (1e-25 g/cm3) minus the above-grade LArTF cylinder, spanning
  6.25 m to 518 m above the origin (the v8 "vacuum above ground" for cosmic
  generation).

The TPC-box and cryostat axis passes through (128.175, 0, 518.5) cm, the
point G4BNB records as `bsim::Location(0, 0, 47000 cm, "MicroBooNE")`
(`NuBeamOutput.cc:169`). The concrete overburden (`volOverburden`, 3.048 m,
never installed) is defined but not placed. Materials: `LAr` 1.40,
`Concrete` 2.3, `Dirt` 1.7, `Polystyrene` 1.06, `Air` 0.001205 g/cm3.
Solids are boxes, tubes, spheres and boolean unions, subtractions and
intersections (2,336 boxes, 709 tubes, 3 spheres, 54 booleans). Published
descriptions: JINST 12 (2017) P02017 (detector) and JINST 16 (2021) P04004,
Sec. 2.1 (the simulated LArTF building).

### Placement in SIREN

`resources/detectors/SBN/SBN-v1/` places the LArSoft world origin at BNB
`(-1.28175, 0, 464.815)` m with no rotation, so the TPC-box centre is the
G4BNB point `(0, 0, 470)` m and the detector origin; the active volume is
exposed as `volTPCActive` with its (-1.55, +0.97, 0) cm offset. The loader
composes a copy from which the `volVacuumSpace` placement is removed
(`microboonev12_nowires_siren.gdml`): composed as is, that box replaces the
SBN composite's atmosphere over the whole site. The world box itself is
dropped (`as_assembly`), so the building and its local ground sit in the site
geology; the local `volGround` takes precedence over the composite's uniform
till within 23 m of the detector.
